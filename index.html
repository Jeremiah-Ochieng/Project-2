Welcome to Riverstone.

Nestled in the vibrant city of Jinja, Riverstone hotel has been a sanctuary of refined comfort and genuine Ugandan hospitality since its founding in 2005. Our mid-range property blends contemporary design with the warmth of East African culture, offering an experience that feels both luxurious and deeply personal.

From our beautifully landscaped gardens and sparkling rooftop pool to our 5 star Restaurant and state-of-the-art conference facilities, Riverstone is more than a hotel — it is a destination.

Restaurant

Savour authentic Ugandan cuisine and international favourites crafted by our award-winning chefs, open daily from 6 am to 11 pm.

Conference Facilities

Three fully equipped meeting rooms seating 10 to 200 delegates, complete with AV equipment, catering, and dedicated event staff.

Pool & Spa

Unwind at our rooftop infinity pool overlooking the Lake Victoria shores, or rejuvenate with a signature treatment at Pearl Spa.

Secure Parking

Our guarded, CCTV-monitored underground car park accommodates up to 80 vehicles, complimentary for all registered guests.

High-speed Wifi

Complimentary fibre-optic Wi-Fi throughout the hotel ensures you stay connected, whether for business or streaming your favourite shows.

Boat Cruise

Comfortable, air-conditioned fast speed boat service, bookable 24 hours a day.
